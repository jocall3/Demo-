# 1. Record Architectural Decisions

Date: 2024-07-23

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The project is undergoing a significant architectural transformation, moving from a monolithic structure to a distributed system of microservices, micro-frontends, and advanced backend patterns (CQRS, Event Sourcing, Service Mesh, etc.). The complexity of the system is increasing substantially.

The rationale behind key architectural decisions is often discussed in meetings, design documents, or transient communication channels like Slack. This knowledge is frequently lost over time, especially as team members join or leave the project.
